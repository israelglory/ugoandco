Corporate Finance & Strategy

Experienced financial leadership for periods of growth, change and complexity.

UGO N & Co. provides senior-level corporate finance and strategy support through fractional, interim and project-based engagements.

Led by Ugochi Nwoga, whose career has included senior leadership roles across finance, strategy, manufacturing, aerospace, international operations and business transformation, engagements combine financial discipline with an operator's understanding of how businesses actually work.

Our Approach

Analysis should lead to action.

UGO N & Co. approaches finance from an operator's perspective.

The objective is not simply to identify what the numbers say, but to understand what is driving them, determine what management can influence and help translate financial insight into practical business decisions.
